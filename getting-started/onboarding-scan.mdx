\## Scanning and Reviewing Your Documentation Structure Before you confirm your documentation skeleton, Syscribe scans your codebase and any connected documentation targets to propose a structure that fits your project. The scan review screen lets you inspect what was found, flag potential issues, and exclude pages you do not want to track. ### What the Scan Finds The scan produces a list of documentation sections, each with: - A \*\*section path\*\* identifying its location in the hierarchy. - An inferred \*\*doc type\*\* — Internal, User-Facing, or API. - A \*\*confidence score\*\* indicating how confident the agent is in its classification. - Any \*\*flagged issues\*\* the agent detected, such as ambiguous page types or missing headings. ### Flagged Issues The agent may flag issues it finds during the scan. You must acknowledge each flagged issue before you can confirm the skeleton. To mark an issue as reviewed, select the checkbox next to it. You can also select \*\*Mark all as reviewed\*\* to clear all issues at once. Common issue types include: - \*\*Low confidence\*\* — the agent could not reliably determine the doc type or section path for a page. - \*\*Missing heading\*\* — a page was found without a clear heading that Syscribe can use as a section title. - \*\*Duplicate path\*\* — two or more pages resolved to the same section path in the hierarchy. ### Excluding Pages To exclude a page from tracking, deselect it in the scan results. Excluded pages are not created in your deployment target and will not receive generated drafts. You can exclude individual pages or entire page trees. ### Rescanning If you make changes to your documentation target — for example, adding or removing pages — you can trigger a rescan from the onboarding screen. Rescanning re-runs the crawler and updates the proposed skeleton. Your existing exclusions are preserved. ### Confirming the Skeleton Once you have reviewed all flagged issues and adjusted your exclusions, select \*\*Confirm\*\* to save the skeleton. The system creates the corresponding documentation nodes and begins the bootstrap process.
