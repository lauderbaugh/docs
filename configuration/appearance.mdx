\## Appearance Settings You can switch between light mode, dark mode, and system default from the appearance settings in the application. Your preference is saved locally and applied every time you open the app. ### Available Themes - \*\*Light\*\* — uses a light colour scheme regardless of your operating system setting. - \*\*Dark\*\* — uses a dark colour scheme regardless of your operating system setting. - \*\*System\*\* — follows your operating system's appearance setting and switches automatically when it changes. ### Changing Your Theme 1. Open \*\*Settings\*\* in the navigation. 2. Select the \*\*Appearance\*\* card. 3. Choose \*\*Light\*\*, \*\*Dark\*\*, or \*\*System\*\* from the theme selector. Your selection takes effect immediately and persists across sessions.
