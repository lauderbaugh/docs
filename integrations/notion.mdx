\# Connecting Notion Syscribe integrates with Notion via Notion's official API. When a draft is approved, Syscribe makes surgical updates to specific sections of your Notion pages — replacing only the content it owns, leaving everything else untouched. This guide walks you through creating a Notion integration, sharing the right pages with it, and configuring the target in Syscribe. --- ## Before you start You will need: - A Notion workspace where you have admin or full-access permissions - A Syscribe account with at least one repo connected --- ## How Syscribe writes to Notion Syscribe uses the Notion API to update your pages directly. Key behaviours: - \*\*Surgical section updates only.\*\* Syscribe does not rewrite entire pages. It finds the specific \`##\` heading (H2 block) that corresponds to a section it owns, and replaces the content beneath that heading — stopping at the next H2 or the end of the page. Everything above, below, and between Syscribe's sections is untouched. - \*\*Sub-pages and databases within a section are never deleted.\*\* If you have embedded databases or child pages inside a section Syscribe manages, they are preserved on every update. - \*\*Syscribe writes native Notion blocks\*\*, not raw text — headings, paragraphs, bulleted lists, code blocks, and callouts are all rendered as proper Notion block types. - \*\*Syscribe only accesses pages you explicitly share with it.\*\* Notion's permission model requires you to grant the integration access to specific pages. It cannot discover or access pages that have not been shared. --- ## How Syscribe reads from Notion Syscribe can also crawl your existing Notion workspace to build your documentation skeleton. The crawler: - Traverses your page hierarchy up to 10 levels deep, following child page blocks. - Extracts the title and text content of each page to build the section tree. - Applies rate-limit delays between requests to stay within Notion's API limits. - Stops traversal at the configured maximum depth to avoid runaway crawls on very large workspaces. You can trigger a crawl from your repository settings to rediscover your existing Notion structure. --- ## Step 1 — Create a Notion integration 1. Go to \[notion.so/profile/integrations\](https://www.notion.so/profile/integrations) and click \*\*New integration\*\* 2. Give it a name: \`Syscribe\` 3. Select the workspace you want Syscribe to write to 4. Under \*\*Capabilities\*\*, ensure the following are enabled: - \*\*Read content\*\* ✓ - \*\*Update content\*\* ✓ - \*\*Insert content\*\* ✓ - \*\*Read comments\*\* — optional, not required
