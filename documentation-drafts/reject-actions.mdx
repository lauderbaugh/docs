\## Rejecting Drafts and Proposals You can reject individual drafts, proposed sections, or all drafts in a pull request at once. Rejection signals that the generated content is not needed or not acceptable for that section. ### Rejecting a Single Draft 1. Select the draft in the section tree. 2. Select \*\*Reject\*\* in the action bar. 3. The draft status changes to \*\*Rejected\*\*. You can regenerate a rejected draft at any time. Regeneration resets the status to \*\*Draft\*\* and produces new content. ### Rejecting a Proposed Section Proposed sections have their own reject action. When you reject a proposal: - The proposed section is dismissed and removed from the section tree. - No new section is created in your documentation. - The proposal cannot be recovered. If you change your mind, you can ask the agent to regenerate content for that section. ### Rejecting All Drafts Select \*\*Reject all\*\* from the action bar to reject every draft in the current pull request that has not yet been deployed. A confirmation prompt appears before the action runs. Deployed drafts are excluded from bulk rejection — they are already published and cannot be rejected retroactively. ### After Rejection Rejected drafts remain visible in the section tree so you have a record of what was reviewed. Their status badge shows \*\*Rejected\*\*. You can regenerate any rejected draft individually if you decide you want content for that section after all.
