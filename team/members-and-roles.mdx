\## Members and Roles You manage your team's access to the documentation system through organisation-level roles and per-repository role assignments. Together these control what each member can see and do. ### Organisation Roles Every team member has an organisation-level role: - \*\*Admin\*\* — full access to organisation settings, all repositories, and all drafts. Admins can manage members and configure routing rules. - \*\*Reviewer\*\* — can review, approve, reject, and deploy drafts on repositories they have access to. - \*\*Viewer\*\* — read-only access. Viewers can see drafts and activity but cannot approve or deploy. ### Repository Roles In addition to their organisation role, members can have a specific role on individual repositories: | Role | What the member can do | |---|---| | \*\*Doc Manager\*\* | Full control over the repository's documentation, including configuration changes. | | \*\*Reviewer\*\* | Can approve, reject, and deploy drafts. | | \*\*Contributor\*\* | Can edit drafts and request reviews, but cannot approve or deploy. | | \*\*Viewer\*\* | Read-only access to that repository's drafts. | ### Changing a Repository Role To update a member's repository role: 1. Navigate to \*\*Settings → Team\*\* and open the \*\*Members\*\* tab. 2. Find the member and expand their repository permissions. 3. Select the new role from the dropdown next to the relevant repository. 4. The change saves automatically. ### Admins Organisation admins automatically have full access to every repository and cannot have their repository role restricted. Their repository role selector is disabled.
